E-commerce Sales & Profit Analysis
Project Overview	

This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, discount impact, and regional trends.
The goal is to extract actionable business insights using Python and data visualization techniques.

 Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profit performance
Identify top-performing product categories
Understand the impact of discounts on profitability
Explore regional and state-level performance differences
Generate business insights and recommendations
Dataset

The dataset contains order-level e-commerce transaction data including:

Profit
Discount
Category & Sub-category
Region & State
Customer segments

Key Insights
. Office Supplies category consistently performs best in both sales and profit, indicating it is the most stable revenue driver in the dataset.
Furniture category generates sales but does not convert this into profit efficiently, suggesting potential issues in pricing strategy or cost control.
The correlation between discount and profit is strongly negative, which suggests that increasing discounts tends to reduce overall profitability.
Sales and profit are not evenly distributed, with certain regions and states contributing significantly more to overall performance.
📌 Recommendations
Reduce excessive discounting to improve profitability.
Focus on high-performing categories like Office Supplies.
Improve pricing strategy in Furniture category.
Optimize regional strategy based on performance distribution
